# Books and frameworks: Social media

The book knowledge base for this plugin is still being prepared by the owner. Until it is added, apply these frameworks from what you know of them, name the source of each recommendation, and summarize in your own words without copying passages.

| Book | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| They Ask, You Answer (Marcus Sheridan) | Building content from the questions buyers ask before purchasing |
| Contagious (Jonah Berger) | Why people share: social currency, triggers, emotion, practical value, stories |
| Made to Stick (Chip and Dan Heath) | Making one idea simple, concrete and memorable |
| Building a StoryBrand (Donald Miller) | Casting the customer as the protagonist and the company as the guide |
| Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook (Gary Vaynerchuk) | Giving value several times before asking, and writing natively for each platform |
| Content Inc. (Joe Pulizzi) | Choosing a narrow content niche and one main channel before expanding |
| Breakthrough Advertising (Eugene Schwartz) | Matching the message to how aware the reader already is |

Books supply the method. What each platform currently rewards comes from a web search with source and date.
