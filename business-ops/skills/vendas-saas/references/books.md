## Pocket summary

- **Bowtie:** the traditional funnel ends at the close. In recurring revenue it opens again afterwards: onboarding, use, renewal and expansion. Winning the customer is half the journey; the profit comes from the other half.
- **Moments That Matter:** each phase has a point where the customer decides to continue. Finding it and giving it an owner is worth more than optimizing the whole phase.
- **Scale after repeatability:** only what already works in a similar way several times can be multiplied. Before that, the job is to learn.
- **Revenue, cash and profit:** a monthly contract becomes revenue gradually while the cost of winning the customer is paid at once. That is why fast growth can squeeze cash.
- **Segment before structure:** cost to serve and contract value change by segment; team design follows that arithmetic.
