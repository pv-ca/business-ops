# Books and frameworks: Ads (paid advertising)

This plugin carries insights drawn from these books, written in its own words; it does not include their text. Apply each framework from what you know of it, name the book behind a recommendation, and never copy passages.

| Book | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Breakthrough Advertising (Eugene Schwartz) | awareness levels and mass desire |
| Scientific Advertising (Claude Hopkins) | test and measure |
| Ogilvy on Advertising (David Ogilvy) | research-led copy |
| $100M Leads (Alex Hormozi) | channels and volume |
| Traffic Secrets (Russell Brunson) | traffic sources |

## Pocket summary

- **Awareness levels (Schwartz):** a reader who does not know the problem, knows the problem, knows solutions exist, knows the product, or is ready to buy needs a different opening each. Most weak ads talk product to someone still at the problem.
- **Desire is channelled, not created (Schwartz):** an ad points a desire that already exists at the product. Find the desire in what customers say before writing.
- **Test and measure (Hopkins):** change one variable, decide the criterion before the test, and keep the version the numbers pick, whatever the opinion in the room.
- **Research-led copy (Ogilvy):** the headline carries most of the result and should promise a benefit the reader cares about. Specific facts persuade more than adjectives.
- **Volume before optimization (Hormozi):** most small companies advertise too little to learn anything. Reach enough people with one offer before splitting hairs.
- **Own the traffic (Brunson):** paid reach is rented. Move people who respond to a list the company controls.
