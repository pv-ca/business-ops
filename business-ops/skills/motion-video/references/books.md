# Books and frameworks: Motion video

This plugin carries insights drawn from these books, written in its own words; it does not include their text. Apply each framework from what you know of it, name the book behind a recommendation, and never copy passages.

| Book | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| The Illusion of Life (Thomas and Johnston) | the 12 principles of animation |
| The Animator's Survival Kit (Richard Williams) | timing and spacing |
| Hey Whipple, Squeeze This (Luke Sullivan) | the creative idea |

## Pocket summary

- **Principles of animation (Thomas and Johnston):** anticipation before an action, ease in and out, follow-through, arcs, staging one idea at a time. In motion graphics, staging and easing do most of the work.
- **Timing and spacing (Williams):** timing is how long a move takes; spacing is how the distance is distributed inside it. The same duration reads as heavy or light depending on spacing.
- **One idea per piece (Sullivan):** find the single thing the video says and cut what does not serve it. A simple idea shown clearly beats several shown fast.
