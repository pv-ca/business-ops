# Books and frameworks: Creators and partnerships

This plugin carries insights drawn from these books, written in its own words; it does not include their text. Apply each framework from what you know of it, name the book behind a recommendation, and never copy passages.

| Book | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Contagious (Jonah Berger) | what makes something shareable |
| Influence (Robert Cialdini) | social proof, authority, reciprocity |
| The Referral Engine (John Jantsch) | referrals and partnerships |

## Pocket summary

- **Why people share (Berger):** it makes them look good, something around them reminds them of it, it stirs a strong emotion, others can see it being used, it is useful, or it travels inside a story. Design the piece for one of these.
- **Persuasion principles (Cialdini):** reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, authority, liking and scarcity. A creator lends social proof and liking; they work only when the creator's audience is the company's buyer.
- **Referrals are designed (Jantsch):** people refer when the experience is worth talking about and when they are asked at the right moment with something easy to pass on. A partner needs the same: a reason, a moment and a ready-made way to refer.
