# Methods and books: sales

The working file is `commercial-sales-playbook.md` in this folder. It restates the methods below in its own words. This plugin carries insights drawn from the books, in its own words, and does not include their text. Rely on the playbook and consult an original book only when the user asks for fidelity to a method or the playbook does not resolve an edge case (section 51 of the playbook). If the user's project keeps a library with the texts, the project instructions say where it is.

| Method | Book | Use it to | Playbook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demand-Side Sales | Demand-Side Sales 101 (Bob Moesta) | Understand why and when a customer decides to change: struggling moment, forces of progress, buyer timeline | Part II |
| Gap Selling | Gap Selling (Keenan) | Diagnose a live opportunity: current state, problem, impact, root cause, future state, size of the gap; qualify, demo, propose, handle price objections | Part III |
| Challenger | The Challenger Sale (Dixon and Adamson) | Complex B2B sale with several stakeholders and a defensible insight that reframes the problem | Part IV |
| Commercial communication | The SaaS Sales Method Fundamentals (Jacco van der Kooij) | Outreach, meetings, questions, listening, follow-up (RRR, ACE) | Part V |
| ABM | Account-Based Marketing (Chris Golec and others) | Named B2B accounts, when value per account justifies concentrated effort | Part VI |

## Pocket summary

- **Selling is helping someone decide on a change.** With no problem worth changing for, there is no sale, only pressure.
- **Demand-Side:** four forces act on every purchase. Discomfort with the current situation and attraction to the new solution push toward change. Anxiety about the new and the habit of the present hold it back. The sale moves when the first two outweigh the last two; reducing anxiety usually pays more than increasing attraction.
- **Gap Selling:** value is the distance between the current state and the future state. A seller who does not know the customer's current state cannot size the gap and ends up arguing about price.
- **Challenger:** teach the customer something they did not see about their own business, tailor the message to each stakeholder and take control of the decision. Only works with an insight backed by evidence.
- **RRR (written message):** relevance to that person, reward for replying, a proportionate request.
- **ACE (meeting opening):** appreciate, check the end time, agree the end goal.

## Selection rules

- Simple, low-ticket or consumer sale: Demand-Side and commercial communication. Challenger and ABM do not apply.
- Neutral research is not selling. To learn about the customer without selling, use the `market-research` skill.
- Diagnose before challenging when the insight has not been validated.
