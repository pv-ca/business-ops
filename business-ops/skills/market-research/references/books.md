# Books and frameworks: Market and customer research

This plugin carries insights drawn from these books, written in its own words; it does not include their text. Apply each framework from what you know of it, name the book behind a recommendation, and never copy passages.

The working method file is `customer-research-playbook.md` in this folder.

| Book | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Deploy Empathy (Michele Hansen) | neutral interviewing, scripts, recruitment; the principal source of the playbook |
| The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick) | asking without bias; past behaviour over opinions |
| Competing Against Luck (Clayton Christensen) | jobs to be done |
| Demand-Side Sales 101 (Bob Moesta) | switching interviews and forces of progress |
| Obviously Awesome (April Dunford) | alternatives and unique value |
| Blue Ocean Strategy (Kim and Mauborgne) | uncontested market space |

## Pocket summary

- **Ask about the past (Fitzpatrick):** what people did last time is evidence; what they say they would do is not. Compliments and hypotheticals are noise.
- **Listen without defending (Hansen):** the interviewer follows the participant's account, asks for the next detail and does not explain, correct or sell.
- **Jobs to be done (Christensen):** people bring a product into their lives to make progress in a specific circumstance. The circumstance explains the purchase better than the customer's profile.
- **Forces of progress (Moesta):** push of the current situation and pull of the new one move a person; anxiety about the new and habit of the present hold them. A switching interview reconstructs the timeline to find each.
- **Alternatives define value (Dunford):** ask what the customer used before and what they would use otherwise. Value is measured against that.
- **Look across categories (Kim and Mauborgne):** study what the customer gave up, what substitutes they use and which features the industry assumes are required, to find what can be removed, reduced, raised or created.
